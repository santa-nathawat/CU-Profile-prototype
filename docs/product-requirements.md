# Product Requirements Document

## Background Context

### What is Thinc.

Thinc. is a student club at Chulalongkorn University that builds technology products for students and the wider community. The club consists of multiple project teams, each working on products that address real-world needs.

### Product Overview & Why We Are Building This Product

A project team within Thinc. wants to help Chulalongkorn University students find teammates for any type of project. Students may have an idea or an opportunity they want to pursue but lack connections with people who have the skills or backgrounds they need.

Students often look for teammates by asking their friends, “Do you know anyone with this background?” This approach limits their search to their existing networks and their friends’ connections. If no one in those networks knows a suitable person, finding a teammate becomes difficult.

For example, Mike, an engineering student, wants to enter a hackathon but needs a teammate with a business background. Meanwhile, Jane, a business student, wants to enter the same hackathon but needs someone with engineering skills. Although their needs complement each other, they may struggle to connect because they do not share mutual connections.

We want to build a platform that helps Chulalongkorn University students find teammates with complementary skills and shared interests. By helping students connect beyond their existing networks, the platform aims to make it easier to form teams and pursue projects together.

## Product Scope

### First Release

The first release helps verified Chulalongkorn University students find teammates, recruit for any type of project, form teams, record project participation, and rate teammates after completing a project.

The requirements below describe agreed behavior for the prototype. Future requirements are listed separately, with unresolved decisions stated explicitly.

### Key Terms

- **Team lead:** The single member responsible for recruitment, membership decisions, and project lifecycle actions.
- **Role capacity:** The maximum number of members who can occupy a particular role.
- **Recruitment status:** Whether a team is accepting new members. Closing recruitment does not mean the project is completed.
- **Project status:** Whether a project has not started, is in progress, is completed, or is cancelled.
- **Eligible rating participants:** The team members recorded when the lead completes the project.
- **Rating window:** The 14-day period beginning when a project is completed.
